# EdVenture Privacy Policy

This policy covers the current offline app, not a future school-account service.

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Developer: Luna Engineering

Privacy contact: kasan.jamil@yahoo.com

## Scope

Luna Engineering provides EdVenture, an educational game for children and families. This policy explains how the current Android app, package `com.edventure.game`, handles information. No account or sign-in is required to play.

## Information used on your device

EdVenture stores the following information locally to remember your game and learning progress:

- Your chosen character name and boy/girl character selection.
- Learning level, completed lessons, and best quiz scores.
- Game coins, snacks, accessory and unlock choices, and other gameplay flags.
- Your character's position and direction within the game world. This is not your real-world location.

Please choose a nickname rather than a child's full name. The app does not need a child's email address, phone number, date of birth, school name, or home address.

Drawing and tracing activities process strokes on your device. The app does not upload these strokes or drawings to Luna Engineering.

## How information is used

Local information is used to resume gameplay, remember lesson completion and scores, and apply earned rewards and character choices. It is not used by Luna Engineering for advertising, tracking, or profiling outside the game.

## Collection and sharing

The current app has no developer-operated backend, account system, analytics service, advertising SDK, or developer-operated crash-reporting service. It does not transmit your character name, saved progress, quiz scores, or drawings to Luna Engineering or to a school. It has no public chat or player-to-player sharing.

Luna Engineering does not sell or rent information from the app, or share local game information with advertisers.

Android backup is enabled. Depending on your device and account settings, Android or the device manufacturer may copy application data to a backup service or transfer it to another device. These services are controlled by the platform provider and are subject to that provider's terms and privacy policy. Luna Engineering does not receive these backups. You can manage available backup options through your device or Google account settings.

## Permissions and device storage

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or real-world location. Its Android manifest includes Internet permission, but the current game does not use a developer-operated online service to send game information.

Saved progress uses the app's local preferences storage. In the browser version, preferences use browser local storage. The app does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Security

On Android, saved preferences are stored in the app's private storage, subject to Android's access controls and the backup behavior described above. Saved preferences are not separately encrypted by EdVenture. Anyone able to access an unlocked device and open the app may see the character name and progress. Protect shared devices with appropriate device locks and supervision. No storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Children's privacy

EdVenture includes nursery, preschool, and primary-school learning activities and is intended to be usable by children. Parents, guardians, or supervising teachers should help children set up a nickname and use shared devices safely.

The current game does not require children to create accounts or submit personal details to Luna Engineering. It does not display advertising or offer real-money purchases. In-game shops use virtual coins earned through gameplay.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support message, contact us using the privacy email above so we can review and address it. Please do not send children's names, school records, or other unnecessary personal information in support requests.

## Retention and deletion

Local progress remains on your device until it is replaced or removed. Starting and saving a new game replaces the active saved game; this is not a deletion of any platform backup.

To remove local Android data, open Settings > Apps > EdVenture > Storage > Clear storage (wording varies by device). Uninstalling normally removes local app data, but a system backup may restore it when you reinstall. Manage or delete backups separately using the options provided by your platform provider.

For the browser version, clear this site's browser storage or site data to remove saved progress. Luna Engineering cannot remotely retrieve or delete your local save. There is no EdVenture account to delete in the current version.

School sign-in, syllabus uploads, cloud progress synchronization, and teacher reporting are not part of the current app. This policy does not authorize future collection for those features.

If we introduce features that change how information is handled, we will update this policy and provide any required notices and obtain any required consent before the new collection begins. The effective date at the top identifies the current policy version.
